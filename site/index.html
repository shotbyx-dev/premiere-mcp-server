How it works

From install to "done" in about fifteen minutes.

  1. 1

    Install it on your PC

    Run one PowerShell installer. It sets up the server, drops tiny bridge panels into Premiere Pro and After Effects, installs ffmpeg, and starts everything when Windows logs on. Windows 10/11, Premiere Pro + After Effects, Adobe signed in — that's the whole list.

  2. 2

    Connect your AI

    The installer creates a private tunnel to your PC (Cloudflare Tunnel — no port forwarding, no open ports) and a secret bearer token. Paste the tunnel URL and the token into your AI client once, and it's paired.

  3. 3

    Direct by chat

    "Cut these clips to the beat, add a cross dissolve between each, export to MP4." Your assistant translates the words into real Premiere commands, executed right on your timeline — and tells you when it's done.

Security & trust

It's your machine. It stays your machine.

🔒 Runs on YOUR hardware

The server binds to 127.0.0.1 only. The only way in from the outside is the encrypted tunnel you create, guarded by your bearer token. No cloud dashboard, no third-party service sitting in the middle.

👁 Full audit log, always

Every single tool call is appended to a local JSONL audit log — timestamp, tool, arguments, outcome. Tokens and sensitive payloads are redacted. If your AI did something, it's in the log.

✋ Destructive actions need you

Your assistant is instructed to confirm before doing anything it can't undo — deleting clips, overwriting exports, closing unsaved projects. And you can stop the whole thing at any moment.

🗑 Revoke anytime

Rotate the token in one command and every client is locked out instantly. Or run the uninstaller: panels, scheduled task, and tunnel service come out, and it's like it was never there.

Honest limits: the bridge gives your AI the same file access Premiere Pro itself has (your project files, media folders, exports). It cannot see your screen or move your mouse — it drives via plugin commands only. Your footage never leaves your machine unless you explicitly ask the AI to generate or fetch something from the web. Get started

Free forever. Open source forever.

  1. Clone the repo on your Windows PC:
    git clone https://github.com/shotbyx-dev/premiere-mcp-server.git
  2. Run the installer in PowerShell:
    .\scripts\install-windows.ps1
    With remote access via Cloudflare Tunnel:
    .\scripts\install-windows.ps1 -TunnelToken "YOUR_TOKEN" -PublicHostname "https://your-tunnel-url.example.com"
  3. Open the bridge panels — Premiere: Window > Extensions > MCP Bridge. After Effects: Window > MCP Bridge Auto.jsx (keep auto-run ON).
  4. Connect your AI client — give it your tunnel URL and the PREMIERE_MCP_TOKEN from your .env, then ask it to verify_premiere_connection.

Lead distribution is Meta Muse: PremierePilot is being submitted to the public connector platform at muse.ai/platform — once listed, connecting is one click. Today, connect via Muse's custom-connector path (paste your server URL in chat — about a minute, no review). ChatGPT connects via developer-mode manual setup (paid plan: Settings → enable Developer mode → chatgpt.com/plugins → + → paste your URL → choose OAuth → create). Claude connects through a custom connector with OAuth. The OAuth 2.1 + Dynamic Client Registration layer that powers these flows is landing now; bearer-token auth already works with any client that can send an Authorization: Bearer header.

FAQ

Asked often, answered honestly.

Does it work with Premiere Pro 2025 / 2026?

PremierePilot targets the current Creative Cloud releases of Premiere Pro and After Effects. Adobe occasionally changes its scripting surface in major releases — when that happens, bridge updates ship with the server, so keep the server (and the bridge panels) updated. If a brand-new version breaks something, open an issue with your app version.

Does my PC need to be on?

Yes. The server, Premiere Pro, and After Effects all run on your Windows PC — there's no cloud copy of your edit. Keep the PC on, plugged in, set to never sleep, and keep the Adobe apps open in a logged-in desktop session. RDP counts as a logged-in session.

Can it see my screen or move my mouse?

No — and that's by design. PremierePilot drives the apps through their official plugin/scripting bridges (CEP panels and ExtendScript), not through screen capture or simulated input. Your AI assistant can read project structure, timeline state, and exported frames — it cannot watch your desktop or click around.

What does it cost?

PremierePilot itself is free and open source (MIT) — no subscription, no per-clip fees, no upsells. You bring your own AI assistant, so whatever that assistant costs (e.g. your Muse plan) is the only thing you pay.

Is my footage uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally on your PC — your media files, your project, your exports. The only exception is when you explicitly ask your AI assistant to generate or fetch something from the web (like an AI-generated clip), and even then only that clip is downloaded to your machine.

Which AI assistants work with it?

Meta Muse is the lead platform: PremierePilot is being submitted to the public connector directory at muse.ai/platform, and until it's listed you can already connect today through Muse's custom-connector path — paste your server URL in chat (about a minute, no review). ChatGPT connects through developer-mode manual setup (paid plan required — see the FAQ below). Claude connects via a custom connector with OAuth. Any client that can send an Authorization: Bearer header works with the current build.

Why isn't PremierePilot in ChatGPT's app directory?

Because it architecturally can't be — and we're being straight about that. OpenAI's directory requires one fixed URL shared by all users, but every PremierePilot user runs their own server on their own PC, so there's no single URL to list. ChatGPT users instead connect through developer-mode manual setup: paid ChatGPT plan → Settings → enable Developer mode → chatgpt.com/plugins → + → paste your server URL → choose OAuth → create. That path is fully supported and works per-user.

What if the AI does something I don't like?

Check the audit log — every tool call is recorded with its arguments and outcome. Your assistant is instructed to confirm destructive actions before doing them, and you can revoke access instantly by rotating the token or uninstalling.
