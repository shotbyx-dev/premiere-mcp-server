Privacy Policy

Your footage stays yours.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

PremierePilot by Shotbyx is a self-hosted product. The server runs on your own Windows PC, drives Adobe apps on that same PC, and there is no Shotbyx cloud service sitting in the middle. That architecture shapes everything in this policy.

What Shotbyx collects: nothing

Shotbyx does not collect, store, or have access to:

  • your footage, project files, timelines, or exports;
  • your prompts or your AI assistant's responses;
  • your bearer token or tunnel credentials;
  • your audit log (it lives on your PC — see below).

There is no account system, no sign-up, and no analytics or telemetry in the software. If analytics are ever added, they will be opt-in and disclosed here first.

What your AI provider processes

When you connect an AI assistant (Meta, OpenAI, Anthropic, or another provider) to your server, your prompts — and the information needed to carry them out, such as clip names, timeline state, and exported frame data — are processed by that provider under their privacy policy. Shotbyx cannot control how your AI provider handles your data, so review their policy before connecting.

What stays on your PC

  • Everything. Media, projects, exports, and the audit.jsonl log of every tool call remain on your machine (default: C:\Shotbyx\).
  • Tokens are generated locally by the installer and never leave your PC except when you paste them into your own AI client.

When data leaves your PC

Only when you explicitly ask your AI assistant to generate or fetch something from the web (for example, an AI-generated clip). In that case the requested media is downloaded to your PC via the server's import tools. Shotbyx itself never initiates such transfers.

Third-party components

The remote-access path uses Cloudflare Tunnel (Cloudflare's software and policies apply to the tunnel itself), and the bridge panels are adapted from community MIT projects (see THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md in the repo). No other third parties are involved.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with an updated date. Continued use of the software after a change means you accept it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/shotbyx-dev/premiere-mcp-server.
